📋 PRESALES FINDINGS · 522 Audits
🔴 Soft Skills Gap Soft Skills 208 errors
Soft Skills is the single biggest presales gap this cycle across ALL channels including Clive — Presale - Outbound (73), Presale - WhatsApp (56), Presale - Inbound (45) and Presale - Chat (20) all show significant gaps. It shows up most on SKU related queries (29) and Jewellery Selection (6) — JCs are not personalising the interaction or building rapport before pitching.
🔴 Probing Insufficient Probing 137 errors
Probing gaps are spread evenly across Presale - Outbound (45), Presale - WhatsApp (44), Presale - Inbound (23) and Presale - Chat (16). On SKU related queries (19) and Jewellery Selection (9), JCs are not asking about budget, occasion or urgency before recommending — a direct hit to conversion quality.
🔴 Sales Closure Missed Sales Closure 101 errors
JCs are not converting interest into commitment. Presale - WhatsApp (41) and Presale - Outbound (36) account for most instances, concentrated on SKU related queries (23) and Jewellery Selection (8) — the query is answered but no CSAT pitch, lead creation, or verbal buying commitment is taken.
🟡 Follow-up Not Done Follow up 54 errors
Follow-up commitments are being missed most in Presale - WhatsApp (27 of 54), largely on SKU related queries (14) — JCs promise a callback or design share and do not close the loop.
🟡 Greetings Not Standard Greetings 51 errors
Greeting misses are heavily weighted to Presale - Outbound (31 of 51) — JCs are skipping the standard opening, self-introduction, or are delayed in greeting the customer, especially on outbound calls.
🟢 Call Closing, Query Ack & Tagging Soft Skills 47 errors
47 call-closing gaps, 36 query-acknowledgment misses and 22 tagging errors — lower volume than the top issues above, but recurring enough to warrant a coaching reminder. Clive channel additionally logged 13 grooming and 9 call/chat-etiquette flags specific to its video-call format.
📞 POSTSALES FINDINGS · 669 Audits
🔴 Empathy Consistently Missing Soft Skills 164 errors
Failed to portray empathy is by far the largest error in postsales this cycle — 105 of 164 instances are in Postsale - Inbound, with Order Status (23) and 15 Days Moneyback (15) the most affected queries. JCs are moving to process information before acknowledging customer frustration.
🔴 Ownership Not Taken Solution 90 errors
90 instances of JCs failing to take ownership of the resolution — 40 in Postsale - Inbound and 29 in Postsale - WhatsApp. Most common on Order Status (15) and Return Pick Up (13), where JCs say they will “check and revert” without a firm commitment.
🟡 Lack of Probing Probing 49 errors
Probing gaps are evenly split between Postsale - WhatsApp (23) and Postsale - Inbound (23), most visible on Return Pick Up (10) queries where the root cause is not fully explored before a resolution is offered.
🟡 Positive Phrases / Ownership Statements Missing Soft Skills 40 errors
40 instances, concentrated in Postsale - Inbound (19) and Postsale - Outbound (16) — most common on Repair Charges (6) and Cancellation (6) calls, where JCs default to policy language instead of reassuring phrasing.
🟡 Retention Not Attempted Solution 33 errors
On Cancellation queries specifically (9 of 33), JCs in Postsale - WhatsApp (17) are processing the request without a genuine exchange or value-retention attempt first — a direct revenue impact.
🟡 Tone Not Inviting Soft Skills 33 errors
33 instances, 22 of them in Postsale - Inbound — JCs sounding flat or rushed, most noticeable on Order Status (5) and Refund (3) calls.
🟢 Incomplete Info, Grammar & Follow-up Solution 26 errors
Incomplete information given (26) and grammatical mistakes (26) round out the next tier, alongside 25 instances of follow-up commitments not honoured — lower volume but recurring enough to flag for coaching.
Recommended Actions
Priority Action Plan
Based on frequency, severity, and customer impact.
📋 Presales — This Cycle
1. Soft Skills & Personalisation — Coach all presales channels, with priority on Presale - Outbound and Presale - WhatsApp, specifically on SKU related queries where the majority of soft-skill gaps occur.
2. Probing & Sales Closure Checklist — Mandate discovery questions and CSAT pitch/lead creation/verbal commitment before ending WhatsApp and Outbound interactions, where these gaps are concentrated.
3. Outbound Greeting Reset — Refresh the standard opening script for Presale - Outbound, which accounts for 31 of the 51 greeting misses.
📞 Postsales — This Cycle
1. Empathy Framework for Inbound — Train Postsale - Inbound on a 3-step empathy response (Acknowledge → Apologise → Act) before sharing process/TAT information, especially on Order Status and Moneyback queries.
2. Ownership Language — Reinforce firm commitments (not "I'll check and revert") across Postsale - Inbound and Postsale - WhatsApp on Order Status and Return Pick Up queries.
3. Retention on Cancellations — Require a documented exchange/value-retention attempt in Postsale - WhatsApp before any cancellation is processed.
